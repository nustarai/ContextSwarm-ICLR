# Provenance

The task statements, metadata, and baseline Lean skeletons under
`benchmarks/matholympiadbench/` were copied from the ContextSwarm repository
snapshot used for this mini artifact.  Their individual `metadata.json` files
retain the upstream source hash/revision information.

The NuRouter/AISW executable, Pi npm package, Lean/mathlib environment, and
any coordinator credentials are external runtime inputs.  They are not
redistributed by this directory.  Verify the upstream benchmark and helper
licenses before publishing a public artifact.
