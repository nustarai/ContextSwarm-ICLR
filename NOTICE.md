# Provenance

The public task statements or metadata, task metadata, and baseline skeletons
under `benchmarks/` were copied from the six canonical ContextSwarm benchmark
bundles at commit `cfed6e508193a0eeebdc56e1e8846f70a0ecc635`: USACO, ICPC
World Finals 2025, PutnamBench, MathOlympiadBench, Clever, and Verina. Their
`manifest.json`, `benchmark_integrity.json`, `PROVENANCE.md` (where present), and
individual task metadata retain the source and revision information.

Production evaluators, hidden tests, oracle outputs, solutions, and Judge-side
packages are not redistributed by this artifact. The USACO bundle is a public
metadata projection only.

The NuRouter/AISW executable, Pi npm package, Lean/mathlib environment, and
any coordinator credentials are external runtime inputs.  They are not
redistributed by this directory. Verify the upstream benchmark and helper
licenses before publishing a public artifact.
